# Small paid pilot

Offer a narrow service: turn a small authorized set of text-based invoices or purchase orders into a structured Excel report, investigate arithmetic differences, and return a reviewed result. A sensible first pilot is 5-20 documents with one or two layouts.

Agree on required fields, row coverage, currencies, rounding, deliverables, timing, price and deletion expectations before receiving data. Pricing and customer demand have not been validated by this project. Do not imply that synthetic demos are completed client work.

Check one redacted sample before quoting a fixed scope. Explain unsupported scans or complex layouts. Obtain explicit authorization before sending any customer data to an external model or service. Default processing is local; files and evidence are not encrypted automatically.

Review every page and all extracted rows. Resolve omissions and uncertain values against the source, retain the original evidence and document corrections. Deliver `report.xlsx` with any unresolved limitations; arithmetic agreement does not prove authenticity, tax compliance or payment approval. Never invent a reviewer identity or client testimonial.
